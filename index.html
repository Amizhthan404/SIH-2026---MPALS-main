Statistical Anomaly Detection Methodology

1. Z-Score Analysis

National-level statistical outlier detection using Z = (x−μ)/σ. MPs with |Z| > 2 are flagged as allocation anomalies.

2. IQR Fencing

Interquartile Range method. Values beyond Q3 + 1.5×IQR or below Q1 − 1.5×IQR are flagged as outliers.

3. Peer Benchmark

State-level peer group comparison. MPs deviating more than 35% from their state mean are reviewed.

4. Milestone Divergence

Compares PFMS fund releases vs recorded physical milestone completion to catch rapid disbursement before work.

5. Asset Verification

Tracks field inspection records for high-value completed projects, flagging unverified assets for audit.
